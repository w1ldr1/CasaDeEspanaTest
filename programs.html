Our Programs

Three Pillars of Cultural Life

Cultural Events, Community Support, and Resources — the three ways Casa de España en Indiana keeps Spanish culture alive and connected across the state.

Program 01

Cultural Events

Cultural Events is the heartbeat of Casa de España en Indiana — the gatherings where Spain's traditions come alive in the Midwest. Since 2004, we've brought flamenco performances, Spanish cinema, and seasonal celebrations to Indianapolis, creating space for the Spanish diaspora and curious neighbors alike to experience Spanish culture firsthand.

  • Flamenco performances and workshops featuring visiting artists from Spain
  • Spanish film screenings and literary discussions
  • Gastronomy nights showcasing regional Spanish cuisine
  • Seasonal celebrations marking Spanish holidays and traditions

Program 02

Community Support

Moving to a new state — or a new country — is easier with people who understand the journey. Community Support connects Spanish-speaking families across Indiana with the language help, social connections, and cultural anchor points that make Indiana feel like home.

  • Peer support and social integration for newcomers to Indiana
  • Connections between longtime Hoosier-Spaniards and recent arrivals
  • Cultural continuity for children growing up bilingual and bicultural
  • A community that understands both sides — the one you left, and the one you're building
